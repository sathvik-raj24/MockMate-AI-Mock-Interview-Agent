# Behavioural round: STAR rubric

Judge the answer as a story, not as a fact. Look for four parts:

- **Situation**: a concrete, specific context (where, when, what was at stake).
- **Task**: the candidate's own responsibility, distinct from the team's.
- **Action**: what *the candidate* did, step by step, using "I" rather than "we".
- **Result**: an outcome, ideally measurable, plus what they learned.

Scoring guidance for this round:

- Missing Result, or a Result with no evidence, caps the score at 5.
- Answers that stay generic ("I am a team player") with no real example score 3 or less.
- Only 8+ if all four parts are present, the Action is specific and the candidate reflects on what they would change.
- Gaps should name the missing STAR part (for example "no measurable Result").
- The hint should point at the missing part without writing the answer for them.

Good behavioural questions are open prompts like "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate" or "Describe a project that did not go to plan". Ask exactly one.
